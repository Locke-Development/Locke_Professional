# WHY CHOOSE LOCKE PROFESSIONAL?
## Evidence-based CLM comparison for prospective engineering clients and employers

**November 13, 2026.** This page evaluates the deployed Locke Professional implementation against the current published offerings of **Ironclad, Icertis, Agiloft, LinkSquares and Juro**. These platforms all offer material AI functionality; no comparison assumes a pre-AI competitor.

> **The product's strongest engineering case:** Locke Professional connects a legal problem to the party's negotiating posture, surfacing issues and alternatives *before drafting*, recording decisions on the matter, assembling from governed content, then carrying the same record into review, approval, signature, storage and obligations. The comparative differentiation is the *composition and explicit decision trail*, not an unsupported claim of universal superiority.

## Competitive strengths acknowledged

- **Ironclad** — sophisticated AI agent suite, detailed playbooks, native Word support, workflow design, analytics and mature end-to-end contracting.
- **Icertis** — large-scale enterprise platform, Copilot and Vera agents, advanced obligation fulfillment and integration into Microsoft, SAP, Salesforce and Workday.
- **Agiloft** — highly configurable no-code workflow engine, Astra/Ask AI, obligation management and increasingly agentic contract operations.
- **LinkSquares** — Analyze/Finalize/Sign/Prioritize combination, citation-backed AI Legal Assistant, playbook-driven in-editor redlining, contract analytics and Word add-in.
- **Juro** — browser-native drafting/collaboration, AI-assisted redlining, built-in e-signature, repository and reporting.

These are **actual market capabilities**, not hypothetical future products. Notably, Ironclad's October 2026 renaming of Jurist to **Contract Review Agent**, Icertis' Vera Copilot and Agiloft's November 2026 specialized agents are included.

## Locke-specific points worth presenting

**1. Decision before prose.** The governed drafting engine distinguishes who the party represents, what risks matter, how alternatives change the commercial balance, and which positions the attorney accepts. Those decisions are stored rather than lost inside a chat.

**2. One legal record, several control layers.** Review findings, source evidence, version history, approval linked to document text, signature artifacts and obligations connect to the contracting workspace.

**3. AI with declared limits.** Ask Locke's response includes stage and navigation actions, grounded contract Q&A can disclose if only an excerpt was read, and model output is validated before action-oriented use.

**4. Engineered extensibility.** A working Word add-in route, read-only API-key model and signed webhook delivery appear in the current source, alongside libraries and specialized domain engines.

## What is not claimed

No independently verified faster legal drafting, more accurate AI, higher savings, stronger security, larger document coverage, lower total cost or better SLA than these competitors. The current 24-family status source labels **18 complete, 4 incomplete, 1 internal and 1 pending commercial decision**; source-level completion is not a live end-to-end certification.
